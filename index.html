Verel's proprietary actives come from botanical biomass.

SAFFROSENSE™ — saffron flower (Crocus sativus)

  • In vivo study: 30 volunteers, 28 days, serum with 2% SAFFROSENSE™.
  • −15.5% wrinkle depth.
  • Firmness improved 8 times and elasticity 2 times versus placebo.

COSMOS Approved · China Approved

BERGALUXE™ — bergamot leaves from annual pruning

  • In vivo study: 30 volunteers, 28 days.
  • +11.6% skin radiance and +8.5% elasticity, both twice the placebo.

COSMOS Approved · China Approved

BERGALUXE™ OC, from the same biomass, is a separate active for oral care. In vitro it reduced Streptococcus mutans and Fusobacterium nucleatum by more than 4.7 log.

OPUNTICELL™ — prickly pear (Opuntia ficus-indica)

  • Moisturising active tested ex vivo with transepidermal water loss (TEWL) measurements.

COSMOS Approved

Chemical characterisation and in vitro tests carried out with the Department of Food and Drug Sciences, University of Parma.

Data refer to the actives. Claims on the finished product are confirmed with dedicated tests on the client's formula.
